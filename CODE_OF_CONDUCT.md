# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in ClickMorph to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Being kind and patient with other people
- Respecting different opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without
  their explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions
and other channels) and when someone officially represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer,
[@avianage](https://github.com/avianage), through a private message or by using GitHub's
"Report content" option. All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the
reporter's privacy will be respected.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that break this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban
anyone whose behavior is inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.
